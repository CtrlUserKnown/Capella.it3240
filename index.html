IT3240 · Capella University FourSides Studio

Over 10 weeks in IT3240 at Capella University, I designed and built a multi-page website for a fictional company: FourSides Studio, a Chicago-based art, film, and open-source technology collective. The course challenged me to think through the full design process, covering what pages a real company would need, how to structure content semantically, and how to layer in interactivity with CSS and JavaScript.

FourSides is modeled after a creator collective, similar in spirit to Glitch, that supports independent artists without the constraints of major corporations. The studio spans film production, graphic design, fine art, and open-source software, all rooted in Chicago's four-sided geography.
