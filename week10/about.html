Who We Are

FourSides Studio started with two friends who kept running into the same wall. The tools available to them were limited, the software was expensive, and nothing was built with the working creative in mind. Rather than accept that, they built something different. What began as a shared frustration became a studio founded on the belief that great visual work does not come from a single discipline. It comes from the collision of many. We are filmmakers, painters, graphic designers, and software engineers working under one roof, driven by a common goal to make work that is honest, lasting, and technically uncompromising.

What Drives Us

Chicago shaped us. The city's four sides represent not just geography, but four distinct cultures, aesthetics, and energies that inform everything we make. We carry that plurality into every project we take on.

What We Stand For

No AI

AI is not a creative tool, it is a shortcut that cuts the creator out of the process. Generative AI models are built on the work of artists who never consented to having their work used as training data, and the output reflects no genuine vision or intent. At FourSides, we do not use AI generation in our work. Creativity comes from people, and we intend to keep it that way.

Freedom

The work we make should not be dictated by budget pressures or the preferences of studio directors. Creatives at FourSides are trusted to follow their vision. The best work comes from artists who are given the room to make decisions, take risks, and see ideas through without compromise.

Free and Open-Source Creative Technology

The programs artists use to make their work should be theirs to understand, modify, and share. Proprietary software puts creative workflows behind paywalls and locks artists into ecosystems they do not control. We develop our tools as free and open-source software so that any creative, regardless of resources, can access, study, and build on them.

Fellowship Among Creatives

No artist grows in isolation. The creative community depends on people sharing knowledge, giving honest feedback, and lifting each other up. FourSides is committed to fostering that culture, inside the studio and in the broader community we are part of. We grow by growing together.

Our Software

A growing part of our practice is the development of creative software tools built entirely in-house and released as free and open-source software. We believe the tools artists use should be transparent, community-owned, and built with a clear purpose. Our software is made to solve real problems that came up in our own workflows, and we release it openly so other studios and independent creatives can use, study, and improve on it.

We are deliberate about what goes into our tools. AI will not be added to our software for the sake of having it there. If a feature does not make the work genuinely better or give the artist more control, it does not ship. We are not interested in trend-chasing. We are interested in building software that respects the person using it.

Our Disciplines

Film Production
Narrative, documentary, and experimental film with an emphasis on visual storytelling.
Graphic Design
Brand systems, editorial design, and print work built on rigorous visual logic.
Fine Art
Original works bridging studio practice and commercial application.
Software Development
Open-source creative tools built for artists, by artists.

Our Process

Every engagement begins with a research phase. We study the subject, the audience, and the medium before a single mark is made. From there, we prototype across disciplines simultaneously, so that the film, the brand identity, and the digital experience are all speaking the same visual language from the start.

We keep our roster small and our standards high. FourSides does not operate as a traditional agency. We function as creative partners, embedded in the work alongside our clients from brief to final delivery.
